Dots that show FORM

Dot size already follows brightness. So shading isn't a setting - you put light & shade in the source, and the dots swell in the light, shrink into shadow.
Flat fill
Uniform colour → every dot the same size → reads flat, like a coin.
→
Shaded
Same circle, lit from upper-left → dots taper to shadow → a sphere.
Cylinder
Curved shading across the width.
Cube
Three flat faces at three brightnesses - stepped dots.
Cone
Curved shading that narrows to the tip.
Brain
Organic shading: the folds become bands of fat dots on the ridges and tiny dots down in the grooves.

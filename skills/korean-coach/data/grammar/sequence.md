# Korean grammar sequence (curated — see ATTRIBUTION.md)

One point per lesson, in order, starting at L5 (after the Unit 0 hangul bootstrap).
Stages map to the level track: A ≈ NIKL grade A words, B ≈ grade B, C ≈ grade C.
Morpheme inventory (particles/endings with RR) lives in `particles-endings.json` — teach from it.

## Stage A — survival core (haeyo-che default; hapsyo-che early for business)
1. Sentence structure: SOV, particles carry the roles, verbs come last and conjugate; politeness
   is grammar, not vocabulary — the speech-level system previewed in one paragraph
2. 이다 copula: X은/는 Y이에요/예요; topic marker 은/는; self-introduction pattern (저는 …)
3. Subject marker 이/가 vs topic 은/는 (contrast, new-info vs about-ness)
4. 해요체 present tense: 아요/어요 vowel harmony, 하다 → 해요 contraction
5. Object marker 을/를; basic SOV sentences with known verbs
6. Negation: 안 + verb; 이/가 아니에요 for nouns
7. 있다/없다 existence and possession; location with 에 / 에서
8. Two number systems: Sino-Korean vs native, and which counts what; core counters 개/명/시/분
9. Past tense 았어요/었어요
10. Future/intent -(으)ㄹ 거예요
11. Question words 뭐/누구/어디/언제/왜/어떻게 (no word-order change)
12. -고 싶다 want to; 주세요 requests
13. **합쇼체 formal register**: -ㅂ니다/습니다, -ㅂ니까/습니까 — meetings, presentations,
    first business meetings (this is why it arrives in Stage A, not C)
14. Subject honorific -(으)시-: 하세요/하십니다; when the *subject* outranks you
15. Directions and means with (으)로; and/with 하고·와/과
16. Ability: -(으)ㄹ 수 있다/없다; 못 inability
17. Long negation -지 않다; nuance vs 안
18. Suggestions and commands: -(으)세요, -읍시다/-(으)ㄹ까요?

## Stage B — connected speech
19. Clause linking -고 (and); sequence -아서/어서 (and then)
20. Reason: -아서/어서 vs -(으)니까 (and why commands need 니까)
21. Contrast -지만; background -는데/(으)ㄴ데 (the everything-connector)
22. Conditional -(으)면; obligation -아야/어야 하다(되다)
23. Permission -아도/어도 되다; prohibition -(으)면 안 되다
24. Progressive -고 있다; resultant state -아/어 있다
25. Noun-modifying verb forms: present -는, past -(으)ㄴ, future -(으)ㄹ (relative clauses)
26. Nominalization -는 것; -기 (and -기 전에 / -(으)ㄴ 후에 before/after)
27. Benefactives -아/어 주다 (and honorific 드리다 preview)
28. Purpose -(으)러 가다/오다; intention -(으)려고 하다
29. Conjecture/volition -겠-; softeners -네요, -지요
30. Experience -아/어 보다 (try); -(으)ㄴ 적이 있다 (have ever)

## Stage C — workplace register
31. Speech-level architecture in full: 해요체 ↔ 합쇼체 switching within one meeting; 반말
    (recognition only — when colleagues use it to you and why you don't reciprocate yet)
32. Humble vocabulary: 드리다/뵙다/여쭙다/말씀; 저희 vs 우리
33. Titles and address: 님, 씨, 직함+님 (부장님/팀장님), never bare 당신 to a superior
34. Indirect speech: -다고/-라고 하다 (reporting, relaying decisions)
35. Passives and causatives (이/히/리/기; -게 하다) — recognition first, production second
36. Formal written style (-습니다 documents vs -(으)ㅁ notes); email frames: 수신/참조,
    greetings, closings (…부탁드립니다, 감사합니다)
37. Meeting language: agenda, opinions (-(으)ㄴ/는 것 같다 hedging, -지 않을까요 suggestion)
38. Large numbers 만/십만/백만/억, prices, percentages, dates — contract-table arithmetic
39. Connectors for argument: 그런데/하지만/따라서/또한; 및/등 (written register)
40. Sound-change mastery pass: 연음, nasalization, tensification, ㅎ-weakening — reading any
    new word aloud correctly without romanization

## Stage D — business proficiency
41+. Negotiation hedging, presentations, KakaoTalk/email register contrast, industry vocabulary
from grade C + operator's domain. Sequenced ad hoc; one point per lesson, guided-discovery format.

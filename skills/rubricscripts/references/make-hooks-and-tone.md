# Make your /hooks and /tone skills

RUBRIC Scripts writes with two skills of your own. /hooks picks how each script opens. /tone makes every line sound like you. Without them your scripts still get written, just in a plain, general voice. Each one takes about 20 minutes to start, and both get better every time you leave comments in the app.

Show this guide to the user when either skill is missing, then offer to build the missing one with them, one step at a time.

## /tone: how you sound

1. **Collect 5 to 10 samples of you.** Transcripts of your best videos, posts you wrote yourself, voice notes. Your own words only, nothing an AI rewrote.
2. **Ask your agent to read them and list what makes them yours:** how long your sentences run, the words you use a lot, how you open, how you close, what you never say.
3. **Save that as a skill** called `tone` (a `tone/SKILL.md` in your agent's skills folder). Start with one short section per register you use, for example "spoken" for scripts and "written" for captions.
4. **Let it grow.** Leave "Teach my /tone" on in the app. Each time you hand comments back, your agent folds what they say about your voice into /tone.

## /hooks: how your videos open

1. **Collect 10 to 20 hooks that worked.** The first sentence of short videos in your niche that did far better than usual, plus your own best ones.
2. **Sort them into shapes.** Ask your agent to group them by how they open: a bold claim, a question, a number list, a problem then a promise, a story. Give each shape a name and two examples.
3. **Save that as a skill** called `hooks` (a `hooks/SKILL.md` in your agent's skills folder), with one rule on top: "write three hooks per idea, each in a different shape".
4. **Let it grow.** When you pick a hook in the app or comment on one, ask your agent to note which shapes you keep choosing.

## Then

Run `/rubricscripts` again on the same ideas. The new scripts use both skills straight away.
